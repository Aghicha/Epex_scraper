What this is (and isn't)

  • Supply is a technology-pooled fleet from ENTSO-E's aggregated capacity — not individual real generating units, so "remove a generator" here means "remove this zone's entire fleet of that technology."
  • Calibration is fit from a small archive (currently usable days for this zone) — a real demonstration of the walk-forward method, not yet a statistically robust fit.
  • Coal/lignite/oil prices are flat placeholders; no free reliable public source was found for them yet.
  • This is an estimation and simulation tool, not a reconstruction of the real EUPHEMIA bid stack — no participant bids are used or assumed.
